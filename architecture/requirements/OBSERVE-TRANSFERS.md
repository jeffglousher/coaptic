# Observe and transfer requirement ledger

This finite ledger owns the accepted UDP origin/client combinations in [#520](https://github.com/jeffglousher/coaptic/issues/520). It assesses current source and named direct proofs, not whole-RFC certification. Fixed and allocated storage share the engine; sync and async share generated protocol bodies. An ordinary response without Observe is an explicitly declined registration, never successful Observe support. Application callbacks own representation identity and current write preconditions.

Original texts: [RFC 7641](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc7641.txt), [7959](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc7959.txt), [8132](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc8132.txt), [9175](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc9175.txt), [9177](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc9177.txt), [7967](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc7967.txt) and [8768](../../knowledge/rfcs/rfc8768.txt). RFC 7967 defines No-Response; RFC 8768 defines Hop-Limit and is not a response-suppression specification.

## Applicable requirements

1. **OBS-IDENTITY — RFC 7641 §§3–4; RFC 8132 §§2.3–2.5.** Relations bind endpoint/token to exact method, ordered query options, content format and FETCH selection body. FETCH requires Content-Format. Refresh/cancel cannot substitute another selection or authenticated owner. Implemented by [ObservePlan/app dispatch](../../src/app/mod.rs), [selection storage](../../src/storage/table.rs) and [client](../../src/app/client.rs). Direct proofs: `selected_observe_retains_get_and_fetch_selection_through_deferred_completion`, `selected_observe_isolates_payloads_declines_oversize_and_reclaims_on_cancel` and cancellation/aggregation cases in [App tests](../../src/app/tests.rs). A requestless snapshot callback deliberately declines selected FETCH/query requests; use `observe_selected` for those capabilities. Oversized selection is a capacity refusal, not an invalid method.

2. **OBS-LIFETIME — RFC 7641 §§3.3, 3.6, 4.1–4.5.** Successful notifications carry a current sequence and stable content format; terminal errors carry no Observe and remove the relation. Explicit deregistration, matched RST, failed CON liveness, replacement and expiry retire only their owner. Implemented in [App progress/notification control](../../src/app/mod.rs). Proof: [supersession](../../tests/observe_supersession.rs), [deferred Observe](../../tests/deferred_observe.rs), [capacity](../../tests/observe_capacity.rs), [classic/Q snapshot lifecycle](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs). Replayed or stale control cannot retire a newer epoch. The latest ambiguous NON MID is retained with bounded lifetime, not an unbounded history.

3. **CLASSIC-OBS — RFC 7959 §§2.4, 2.6.** Observe concerns the entire resource; every Content notification starts at block zero. The server remembers requested maximum SZX and may downshift to actual packet capacity. Request M is ignored, as required for Block2. Nonzero initial NUM does not create a block-specific observation: admitted registration normalizes the initial notification to NUM0. Follow-ups have no Observe and retain representation/selection checks. Direct/deferred, protected/unprotected proof: `nonzero_initial_observe_registers_whole_resource_direct_and_deferred` and preferred-size/snapshot cases in [deferred_observe_block2](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs). Invalid SZX/option encoding remains refused.

4. **Q-OBS-EPOCH — RFC 9177 §§4.4–4.6.** Every initial/unsolicited Q body and Continue window uses one ETag, Size2 and Observe epoch. Missing-block requests/responses omit Observe. Initial nonzero selections retain a complete immutable snapshot; subsequent notifications restart at NUM0. Both M=0 and M=1 selections are supported directly and deferred. Proof: `q_observe_retains_epoch_snapshot_and_last_block_for_recovery`, `q_observe_first_block_preference_registers_and_continues_without_redispatch`, and the nonzero test in [deferred_observe_block2](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs). Selected nonzero offsets refuse if their requested SZX cannot encode; they are never silently reinterpreted at another byte offset. Retry with a fitting SZX. NUM0 negotiation may downshift.

5. **Q-OBS-CON — RFC 9177 §§4.4–4.5, 7.** All blocks of one body preserve CON/NON type. CON notifications progress one accepted send and exact ACK at a time under NSTART; cancellation cannot skip an unsent range. After completion, missing-block recovery supports one bounded MAX_PAYLOADS_SET, including M=1 suffixes and sparse multiple M=0 selectors. The existing window bitmap retains that selection and prevents accidental continuation into another set; no additional buffer/table is introduced. Proof: `con_q_observe_crosses_windows_exact_acks_terminal_and_next_epoch`, `con_q_observe_cancelled_continuation_retries_same_block_without_skipping`, `con_q_observe_recovery_retains_bounded_selection_until_exact_ack` in [CON tests](../../tests/support/q_observe_con.rs). Pending original/recovery CON work and selections spanning sets receive bounded 5.03 with Max-Age 1 (subject to No-Response and authenticated transport availability); the client can retry after ACK progress and select sets separately. This is a bounded scheduling profile, not a claim that such requests are malformed.

6. **UPLOAD-IDENTITY — RFC 9175 §3; RFC 7959 §§2.5, 2.7; RFC 9177 §§4.3, 4.6.** Fragments retain Request-Tag, method, selection, validators and required Size1. Different owners/tags cannot cross-complete; duplicate fragments do not repeat application effects. Q missing reports retain the exact condition and immutable identity. [RequestBinding](../../src/storage/block.rs), [block receive](../../src/app/mod.rs) and [client](../../src/app/client.rs) implement the boundary. Proof: [block sweep](../../tests/block_sweep.rs), [If-Match](../../tests/if_match_upload.rs), [Q FETCH upload](../../tests/q_fetch_etag_upload.rs). Echo freshness/reachability policy is separate from durable effect deduplication; it does not replace Request-Tag or an application receipt journal.

7. **UPLOAD-WRITE-VALIDATOR — RFC 7959 §2.10 and RFC 7252 §5.10.8.** If-Match survives classic/Q upload and is checked against the current representation at completed dispatch. If-None-Match belongs only on NUM0; changing it on a duplicate NUM0 or adding it later is refused before body mutation. The retained NUM0 condition survives Q reordering and retransmission. Proof: [If-Match](../../tests/if_match_upload.rs), [classic If-None-Match](../../tests/if_none_match_upload.rs), [Q If-None-Match](../../tests/q_if_none_match_upload.rs), including two competing creators and fresh recovery. Handlers must atomically evaluate/update their own resource; reassembly is not an application transaction engine.

8. **FETCH-VALIDATOR — RFC 8132 §2.3.2.** ETag validates the selected response, including FETCH payload; it is not a write precondition. Complete classic/Q fragmented FETCH and oversized selected follow-ups retain the selector and validator; changed/omitted validators or foreign owners cannot reuse another representation. Proof: [classic FETCH](../../tests/fetch_etag_upload.rs), [Q FETCH](../../tests/q_fetch_etag_upload.rs), [deferred validator](../../tests/deferred_fetch_validator.rs). Fragmented ETag on other upload methods is an explicitly unsupported builder combination, not an If-Match substitute or a universal wire-format error. No accepted requirement gives that combination write-validator semantics. A 2.03 Valid registration sends no empty-body block slice and retains classic/Q notification mode and SZX; `initial_valid_observe_retains_classic_and_q_notification_preferences` in [deferred_observe_block2](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs) covers direct/deferred transitions to a changed representation.

9. **NO-RESPONSE — RFC 7967 §§2–2.2; RFC 9177 §§4.3, 7.** Masks suppress response classes, not transport ACKs. Reserved bits do not change supported registration. Observe registration requires interest in success; error-only masks and suppressed deregistration/terminal errors work. Classic Block1 requires interest in 2.31 Continue. Q uploads retain interest in 4.08 missing-block reports; CON progress uses ACKs and NON success suppression uses bounded pacing. These protocol-progress restrictions are pre-I/O builder refusals, not claims that RFC 7967 universally forbids the masks. Proof: [classic masks](../../tests/no_response_upload.rs), [Q masks](../../tests/q_no_response_upload.rs), [CON Q masks](../../tests/q_con_no_response_upload.rs), [deferred Observe masks](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs). A missing suppressed response does not prove application success.

10. **HOP-LIMIT — RFC 8768 §§1.1, 3.** The origin API parses the option through [Request::hop_limit](../../src/app/request.rs); this does not implement forwarding, decrement, loop detection or proxy diagnostics. Proxy/cache roles are unsupported contribution opportunities in this milestone. Those requirements are inapplicable to this accepted origin Observe/transfer profile, not completed by option decoding.

A payloadless initial Valid may omit Content-Format. Explicit Accept establishes
its format immediately; otherwise the format remains unknown until the first
visible Content. Unknown format differs from a representation known to omit
Content-Format. Later Valid responses retain a known binding. Admission refusal
before sending leaves the binding unknown; an ambiguous send or cancellation
after a committed Q packet preserves it. The existing observer policy byte holds
this state; no retained buffers or tables are added. Direct proofs include
`valid_without_format_preserves_known_absence_and_accept_constraints`,
`first_content_format_binds_on_ambiguous_send_but_not_admission_refusal` and
`cancelled_q_first_content_binds_only_after_a_packet_commits` in
[deferred_observe_block2](../../tests/deferred_observe_block2.rs).

Automatic GET Block2 retrieval omits the original request's cache-validation
ETag. Response ETags still establish representation identity; explicit
preconditions and non-GET validators, including FETCH selection, remain retained.
`download_followups_retain_conditions_and_fetch_selection` in
[App tests](../../src/app/tests.rs) covers that distinction.

The client receives CON Q bodies through exact empty acknowledgements rather
than concurrent NON-style next-window pulls (RFC 9177 appendix A.2). Duplicate
payloads with the same retained identity are ignored without replacing body or
inbox ownership, as required by section 4.4. The two-epoch, 704-byte
`con_qblock2_observe_crosses_windows_without_pull_and_keeps_duplicate_blocks`
regression in [App tests](../../src/app/tests.rs) crosses both ten-block
boundaries and repeats boundary/final blocks. Shared sync/async driving adds no
state or capacity. Core #627 corrects the independent native counterexample.

A changed-selector ordinary follow-up can receive 4.00 without retiring either
registered selection. The [refusal/pacing regression](../../tests/observe_selection_refusal.rs)
retains both relations, observes the same-endpoint NON hold, then delivers the
second notification at the 3,000 ms deadline. A two-second fixture wait was not
proof of relation loss; companion #112 corrects that wait without changing the
server.

## Completion and independent gates

The implementation gate is the applicable rows above, including their explicit capacity/refusal/recovery contracts. Source tests are relevant proof; they are not independent interoperability or current-device execution. No ordinary-response fallback closes an accepted registration row. Any future expansion of the retained selector/window capacities must account for the entire App, scratch, security and transport lifetimes rather than increasing a table in isolation.
